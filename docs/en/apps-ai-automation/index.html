08 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions before you start.

Will my data end up in some outside AI? +

Not without you knowing. We use providers with servers in the European Union and tell you in writing which data goes through each service.

Do I have to change the software I use? +

Usually not. We connect what you already have: email, spreadsheets, invoicing software or calendar.

What happens if something fails? +

Every automation alerts you when it can’t finish a task and leaves it for a person. With maintenance, we respond within 24 h.

How long until it’s up and running? +

A simple automation takes 2 to 4 weeks. A custom app depends on the scope we agree in the assessment.

What if I don’t save the hours you predicted? +

You’ll see it in the pilot measurement, before anything is expanded. If the result isn’t worth it, we stop there.

Why trust a new company? +

Because the assessment is free, the price is fixed and the code is yours. You don’t depend on us to keep running.
