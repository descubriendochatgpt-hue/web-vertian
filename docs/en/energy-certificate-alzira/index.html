03 · WHAT IT IS

A letter you must show to sell or rent.

The certificate measures how much energy the property needs and how much CO₂ it emits, and sums it up as a letter from A to G. It is mandatory to sell or rent, and the label must appear in the listing. Tap a letter to see what it means.

09 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What people in the Ribera usually ask us.

Do you really need to visit? I’ve seen online certificates with no visit. +

Yes. Royal Decree 390/2021 requires the technician to visit the property. A certificate issued without a visit can be rejected and exposes you to a fine.

How long does it take overall? +

We reply within 24 h, visit within 1 to 3 days and deliver within 5 working days of the visit. With the urgent option, 48 h after the visit.

What if I get a bad rating? +

You can still sell or rent. The certificate includes improvement recommendations sorted by cost, useful if you plan to apply for renovation grants.

Do I have to be there for the visit? +

It doesn’t have to be you. Anyone who can let us in will do: a relative, the tenant or your estate agent.

What documents do I need? +

The address and, if you have it, the cadastral reference (it’s on the IBI property tax bill). Invoices for new windows, boiler or insulation help improve the result.

Why trust a new company? +

Because everything we say can be checked: tax ID, registered office, liability insurance and a registered technician. And because the price is fixed in writing before the visit.
