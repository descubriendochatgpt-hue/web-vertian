03 · HOW IT WORKS

You earn a commission every time someone buys through your link.

Brands pay for sales, not visits. So the work comes down to three things: choosing programs that pay well, writing content that helps people decide and measuring which links make money.

The affiliate accounts are in your name. Commissions are paid to you. We charge a fixed fee you see before we start.

WHAT WE MEASURE
Revenue, cost, margin, clicks and conversion
WHAT WE DON’T
Followers, likes or reach without sales
TRANSPARENCY
Sponsored links are disclosed, as the law requires

08 · FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions before you start.

When will I start seeing revenue? +

We won’t give you a date we can’t guarantee. From the first month you see clicks and conversions for every link in the dashboard, so you know whether it’s on track.

Do I need a website? +

It helps, but it isn’t required. We also work with newsletters, social media and video channels. The audit tells you which channel makes most sense.

Who gets paid the commissions? +

You do. The affiliate accounts are in your name and brands pay you directly. We only invoice the agreed fee.

Is there a lock-in? +

No. It’s month to month and you can cancel with 30 days’ notice. Published content remains yours.

Is it legal? Do links need to be disclosed? +

Yes, it’s legal, and yes, advertising must be disclosed. All the content we produce identifies affiliate links.

Why trust a new company? +

Because you don’t have to do it blindly: you see the data in your dashboard, there’s no lock-in and everything we create stays in your name.
